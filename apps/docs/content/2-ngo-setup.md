# Setting up an NGO

1. Create the NGO at **For NGOs** on any site, then confirm the email.
2. Staff, **Legal and payments**: enter legal name, PAN, 80G and 12A numbers.
3. A platform administrator reviews and sets the NGO to **Active**. Until then it cannot take donations.
4. **Legal and payments**: add your own Razorpay key, secret and webhook secret. Copy the webhook address shown there into Razorpay and turn on payment, refund and subscription events.
5. **Website**: headline, colours, contact details, languages. Impact figures need a source, because they are public claims.
6. **Pages**: publish your own About, Privacy, Terms and Refunds pages. Built-in drafts are shown until you do; have them reviewed.
7. **Team**: invite staff. Nobody can give access they do not have themselves.
8. Make a small real donation and refund it before announcing the site.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| NGO Administrator | Everything in their NGO |
| Manager | Campaigns, donors, CRM, programmes, learning, content |
| Finance | Donations, refunds, receipts, ledger, invoices |
| Auditor | Read and export finance records only |
| Teacher | Courses, marking, library |
| Content Creator | Pages, social posts (someone else approves) |
| Field Worker | Visits and attendance, also offline |
| Donor, Volunteer, Beneficiary, Student, CSR Partner | Their own account pages |

## Foreign donations

Refused by default. They open only when the NGO has a valid FCRA number, a platform administrator has recorded the check, and a separate FCRA payment account is connected.

## Receipts and certificates

Names, addresses and campaign titles print in all 13 languages of the platform, including Urdu right to left. The fixed wording stays in English. Receipts issued earlier print correctly when downloaded again, because the original name was stored.
